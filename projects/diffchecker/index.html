diffchecker.local

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing leaves this device.

Shortcuts & legend

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl/⌘ + EnterCompare
  • SSwap original ⇄ changed
  • VToggle side-by-side / unified view
  • NJump to next change
  • PJump to previous change
  • DCopy diff output
  • MCopy merged result
  • ?Show/hide this panel

Single-letter shortcuts only fire when you're not typing in a text field, so they won't interfere with pasting or editing.

Colors

  • Removed text — in the diff view, and as "keep original" in the merge assistant
  • Added text — in the diff view, and as "keep changed" in the merge assistant (the default)
  • Merge assistant only: keeping both versions in the output

Merge

Click a highlighted change to cycle it through changed → original → both. Whole added/removed lines get a single choice instead.

Paste text on both sides, then hit Compare.
